Party Tray Express - Website Plan

Your affordable choice for delicious party trays and catering packages.

Site Name

Party Tray Express

The name was selected because it clearly represents a business that provides fast, convenient, and reliable party tray ordering services for families, offices, and special occasions.

Optional Domain: partytrayexpress.com

Site Purpose

Party Tray Express is designed to promote a small food business that offers affordable party trays for gatherings, corporate events, birthdays, and celebrations. The website will provide menu information, tray options, prices, serving sizes, delivery details, and an online inquiry form for customers.

Scenarios

  • What party tray package is best for 20 to 30 guests?
  • Which areas are included in the delivery service?
  • How much does each party tray cost?
  • How can I place an order for my upcoming event?

Color Scheme

Teal (#008080)

Used for the header, navigation menu, headings, buttons, and footer.

Light Coral (#FF7F50)

Used for call-to-action buttons, highlights, borders, and accents.

Light Mint (#F0FFFA)

Used as the page background and section backgrounds to create a fresh and clean appearance.

Typography

Poppins will be used for headings, navigation, and section titles.

Open Sans will be used for body text, descriptions, forms, and general content.
